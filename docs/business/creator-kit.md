## 1. 15–30s Short-Form Clip Template

| Beat | Time | Visual | On-Screen Text / Audio |

| **1. Hook** | 0–3s | Friends huddled around a table, phones out, bottle spinning on screen | *"Truth or Dare with an AI Owl that judges your dares."* |

## 2. Creator Kit Assets & Guidelines

### Visual Identity ("Evening Table")
- **Colors:** Linen (`#F6F1E9`), Ink (`#23201C`), Truth Teal (`#2E5E6B`), Dare Terracotta (`#C4552D`), Brass (`#B8893B`).
- **Mascot:** Top-hatted owl (**Vowlet**) — idle, judging, approve, disapprove, and roast poses.
- **Typography:** Cormorant Garamond (headlines/titles), Inter (UI/captions), Kalam (handwritten chit callouts).

### Content & Safety Rules (Mandatory for Creators)
1. **18+ Audience:** Creators must tag/frame content for 18+ friend groups.
2. **Safe Dares Only:** Never film dares involving alcohol/drugs, driving, heights, fire, trespassing, nudity, or public harassment.
3. **Consent First:** Everyone visible in the clip must consent to being filmed before hitting record.
4. **No Fake Claims:** Do not promise native app-store listings during Private Beta; direct viewers to the web/PWA link.
