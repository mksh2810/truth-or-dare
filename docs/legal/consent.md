# Vowlet — First-Run Consent & Table Rules

**Product:** Vowlet (*"A little truth, a little trouble"*)  
**Applies to:** First-run consent screen (Decision 14, Fix Checklist `B10`)

---

## 1. 18+ Self-Declaration (Not Age Verification)
By tapping **"I Agree & Enter"** on the first-run screen, you self-declare that:
- You are **18 years of age or older** (or the age of majority in your jurisdiction, if higher).
- Vowlet relies on your **honest self-declaration** and does not perform government-ID or biometric age verification.
- You will not invite or permit anyone under 18 to participate in a Vowlet room.

## 2. Table Rules & Safe Play
- **Consent & Respect:** Every player has the right to **Skip** any Truth or Dare at any time (in-game Skip costs `-30` session points, never real-world penalties). Never pressure, coerce, or shame another player.
- **No Alcohol or Drugs:** Vowlet does not include drinking prompts and strictly prohibits dares involving alcohol, drugs, or substance consumption.
- **No Harm or Illegal Acts:** Do not write or perform dares involving physical harm, trespassing, driving, fire, heights, ingestion, nudity, or public harassment. (See [Safe Dares Policy](./safe-dares.md).)
- **Anonymity & Sportsmanship:** Chits are anonymous during gameplay. Vote honestly (`Approve` / `Reject`) based on whether the performer gave a good-faith effort.

## 3. Video Call & Recording Notice
- **Others Can Record Video Calls:** If your group uses an external video call link (e.g., Google Meet, WhatsApp, Discord) or future in-app video features, **other participants can record audio, video, or screenshots of the call** using device or third-party tools outside Vowlet's control.
- Only share or perform what you are comfortable having seen by everyone in your room.
