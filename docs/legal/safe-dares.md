# Vowlet — Safe Dares Policy

**Last Updated:** Private Beta Draft  
**Applies to:** All custom chits, curated prompts, and in-person / remote gameplay.

---

## 1. Core Rule: Consent & Safety First
Vowlet is built for **harmless fun among 18+ friends**. Every player always has the right to tap **Skip** on any prompt without shaming. A dare is never an obligation.

## 2. Strictly Prohibited Dares (Zero Tolerance)
You must **never** write, submit, or encourage dares involving:
1. **Alcohol, Drugs, or Substances:** Drinking games, shots, smoking, vaping, or consuming any intoxicating or unknown substance.
2. **Physical Harm or Pain:** Slapping, hitting, eating spicy/inedible items, holding breath, extreme cold/heat, or any risk of injury.
3. **Ingestion of Non-Food or Allergens:** Eating raw/spoiled items, toothpaste, soap, or anything unsafe.
4. **Heights, Fire, Weapons, or Vehicles:** Climbing, jumping, open flames, sharp objects, or driving/operating machinery.
5. **Trespassing or Illegal Acts:** Entering private property, shoplifting, vandalism, prank-calling emergency services, or breaking any law.
6. **Nudity or Sexual Acts:** Stripping, flashing, non-consensual touching, or sexually explicit acts.
7. **Public Harassment or Non-Consensual Pranks:** Harassing strangers, filming/photographing people without consent, or naming real non-participants.
8. **Self-Harm or Reckless Challenges:** Any dare encouraging self-injury or emotional distress.

## 3. Allowed Dare Types
When writing a dare, choose one of the two Safe Dare categories:
- **Instant:** Playful, room-safe physical or performance dares (e.g., *"Do your best dramatic movie trailer voice for 20 seconds"*).
- **Social / External:** Lighthearted, consent-safe social actions (e.g., *"Send a wholesome compliment to the last friend you texted"* — never harassing, never involving strangers or real non-participants' private info).

## 4. Enforcement
- Custom chits are screened by deterministic English + Hinglish filters and the fail-closed Judge Owl pipeline.
- Unsafe chits are rejected with a friendly Owl explanation (`NOPE`).
- Repeated violations or abusive behaviour can be reported and blocked via the in-app **Report / Block** sheet.
